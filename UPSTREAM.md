## World data

- Upstream: https://github.com/OpenDAoC/OpenDAoC-Database, pinned at `98738ab8495f7e0a17412318e7c7c32a96d0eb09`
  (2026-09-29). A compressed copy is bundled in `world/upstream-db/` (with its GPL LICENSE);
  `tools/fetch-upstream-db.sh` unpacks it (or downloads, if it's missing) and checks a content hash
  over the `.sql` files.
- `world/patches/200-*.sql` turn it into Offline DAoC 0.33's pristine Classic + SI world. They were
  generated by `tools/world/derive-033-delta.py` and verified to reproduce that world exactly (0
  differing tables). Moving the DB pin means re-running that script and re-verifying.
